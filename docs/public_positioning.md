# Public Positioning

## Category wedge

**Account Intelligence OS** is the first wedge into a larger category: **AI Decision Intelligence**.

The immediate pain is simple: customer-facing teams do not know which accounts changed, why it matters, who to contact, what to say, and what evidence supports the recommendation.

The broader platform thesis is that every AI workflow eventually needs:

- decision capture
- runtime observability
- operational memory
- policy and governance
- outcome feedback
- continuous improvement

Account intelligence is a practical place to prove this.

## One-line description

Open-source agent workflows for evidence-backed account intelligence, daily signals, meeting prep, and product feedback.

## Product narrative

Account teams do not need more dashboards. They need an always-on intelligence system that watches signals, builds context, validates evidence, and recommends the next best action.

## What makes this different

Most tools start with contact enrichment or CRM automation. This project starts with an evidence bundle and a quality gate.

The core question is not “Can we generate a nice account summary?”

The core question is:

> Can we prove why this recommendation is being made?

## Ideal early users

- Solutions architects
- sales engineers
- account executives
- customer success teams
- product managers
- founder-led B2B teams
- revenue operations teams experimenting with agents

## Public-safe language

Use:

- public sources
- private connectors
- customer-owned data
- account isolation
- evidence-backed recommendations
- optional telemetry store
- optional trace integration

Avoid:

- references to internal employers
- internal channel names
- proprietary customer examples
- private connector details
- claims based on non-public information
