# Blogging Playbook

## Goal

Use the public repo to create a category narrative before building a full product.

## Initial series

1. Why Account Intelligence Is the Perfect First Agentic Workflow
2. How Four Agents Can Monitor Enterprise Accounts
3. Evidence Bundles: The Missing Primitive in AI Workflows
4. Why AI Agents Need Quality Gates
5. From Account Intelligence to Decision Intelligence
6. Why Observability Is Not Enough for Enterprise AI Agents
7. The Rise of the AI Decision Graph
8. Building a Privacy-First AI Control Plane

## Writing style

- Write from field experience, not hype.
- Use diagrams and concrete workflows.
- Avoid naming private customers or employers.
- Use synthetic examples.
- Show artifacts: schemas, prompts, quality gates, run outputs.

## CTA

Each post should point to the repo and ask readers to test the workflow against one public account.
