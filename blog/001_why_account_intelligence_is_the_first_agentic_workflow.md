# Why Account Intelligence Is the Perfect First Agentic Workflow

Most teams are trying to build AI agents by starting with generic automation.

I think account intelligence is a better starting point.

It has clear inputs, clear users, clear business value, and a natural quality gate: every recommendation should be traceable to evidence.

The workflow is simple:

```text
Collect signals -> Normalize evidence -> Reason about priority -> Recommend next action -> Capture outcome
```

That pattern is bigger than sales. It is the foundation of AI Decision Intelligence.

If an agent recommends contacting a customer, prioritizing an account, or changing a product roadmap, we should know:

- what evidence it used
- what confidence it had
- what policy checks passed
- what action it recommended
- whether the recommendation helped

Account intelligence gives us a practical way to build and test those primitives in the real world.
