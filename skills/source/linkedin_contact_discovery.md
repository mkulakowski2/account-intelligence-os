# Skill: linkedin_contact_discovery

## Purpose

Find and classify relevant contacts.

## Targets

- CTO / CDO / VP Engineering / VP Data
- Head of Platform / Director Data Engineering
- Staff / Principal Data Engineer
- SRE / Platform Engineer
- ML / AI Platform / LLMOps roles
- BI / analytics leaders where relevant

## Rules

- Fill to 20 contacts when possible.
- Mark contacts as VERIFIED only when employer alignment is clear.
- Mark role-fit prospects with no signal as LinkedIn Prospect.
- Do not use unverified contacts for outreach.
